Why the drafter is locked
It is the one role whose context the engine bounds mechanically. You can change its model; you cannot widen what it reads. That restriction is the product.
Why the continuity reviewer is not
To catch a leak it has to know the secret the drafter never saw. It is the only role that reads both sides — so it is the only one that must never write prose.
Everything is optional
Set any role to manual and you do it yourself. All seven manual is a valid setup: the engine still checks every accept.
